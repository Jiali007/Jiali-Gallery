Exhibit I — Self Portrait · Artist Statement
She does not merely observe numbers; she interrogates them.

Based in Switzerland and pursuing her Master's at HEC Lausanne, she has built a professional identity at the intersection of data-driven strategy and real-world execution. Her dual-continent background in Economic Statistics and Financial Management allows her to navigate between granular mechanics and broad business ecosystems.

What defines her is an insatiable curiosity that extends beyond the classroom into investment syndicates and competitive arenas. She views the financial markets as a living laboratory for how businesses compete and evolve.

A self-driven practitioner, she is drawn to environments where analytical rigour meets tangible influence. Beyond the technical, she possesses a rare tilt toward financial storytelling — refining the art of translating intricate economic concepts into compelling narratives. In her world, data informs, but clarity inspires.
